FAQ

Is this the same as “sync” (Dropbox/Drive)?
No. Sync propagates deletions and ransomware. Backups keep historical versions so you can roll back.

Is it fully automated?
Yes. The backup job runs on a schedule. If it fails, you’ll get a desktop notification and a clear log command.

Can you prove restores?
Yes: we do an actual restore drill, not just “it says success.”
